Heating and Air Conditioning Service Across San Francisco

We service every type of heating and cooling equipment you'll find in local homes and businesses, from central air conditioners and gas furnaces to heat pumps and ductless mini-splits. It makes no difference whether the building is a starter home, a condo, or a storefront. Most homeowners we meet need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. When Your System Fails in San Francisco

Call us with a problem and a real person answers, not a phone tree. The majority of calls are scheduled within a day, and true emergencies, an AC that quits in a heat wave or a furnace that goes out on a bitter night, jump to the top of the schedule. We keep the process simple so nobody is stuck waiting for a callback.

The urgent calls we run tend to follow a pattern. On the cooling side, it's warm air from the vents, an outdoor unit that hums without starting, or an evaporator coil iced over. On the heating side, it's a furnace that ignites and drops out. Year-round, it's breakers that trip whenever the system starts, plugged condensate drains overflowing into the furnace cabinet, and thermostats that have quit. Because our trucks are stocked with capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, universal control boards, and fittings, the majority of these calls get fixed on the first trip, at a price you approve before we start.

Two situations call for quick action from you before our technician gets there. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: switch the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can flood the drain pan and hurt the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can prevent a much larger repair.

How San Francisco Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Most mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or a service call every season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and much easier on you than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

Small problems don't stay minor when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a few miserable nights.
